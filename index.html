07FAQ

Good questions

Can’t find what you’re after? Call 0491 570 156 or email hello@crystalcarclinic.com.au.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. Our van carries its own water tank and a near-silent power supply. We use rinseless and low-water techniques that are kind to Perth’s water supply and safe for driveways, apartment car parks and office basements.

How long does a detail take?

A Crystal Wash takes about 1.5 to 2 hours, a Signature Detail 4 to 5 hours, and a Paint Enhancement or ceramic coating a full day. Multi-year ceramic coatings may need a second day for curing. We’ll confirm timing with your quote.

What happens if it rains or it’s 40°C?

We work under a portable shade gazebo and monitor panel temperatures carefully. If the weather makes a quality finish impossible, we’ll reschedule at no cost. Ceramic coatings are best done undercover, such as in a garage or carport.

What’s the difference between a sealant and a ceramic coating?

A sealant sits on top of the paint and lasts months. A ceramic coating chemically bonds with the clear coat, forming a harder, glassier layer that lasts years, resists UV and chemical etching, and makes washing dramatically easier.

How do payment and deposits work?

Detailing packages are paid on completion by card, Apple Pay or bank transfer. Ceramic coatings need a 20% deposit to secure your day. Your estimate is confirmed once we’ve seen the vehicle’s condition, with no hidden extras.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. Fortnightly or monthly Crystal Washes save 10% and keep coated cars performing at their best. Mention it in your quote request and we’ll set up a recurring slot.
